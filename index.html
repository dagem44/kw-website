01 / THE COMPANY

Production depth.
Human attention.

KW Printing and Advertising was established in 2015 by founder and General Manager Kahsay W/Silassie. The company grew by doing difficult jobs well: tight timelines, demanding production and work tailored around each client's idea.

Today, KW combines printing knowledge, label printing, modern machinery, fabrication, advertising and service into one operating platform. The goal is simple: reliable perfection.
